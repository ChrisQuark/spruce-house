DESIGN BASIS · R02

What the builders are seeing.

This is a dimensional visual brief for discussion and estimating. It is not a structural, fabrication or construction drawing.

From the manufacturer’s sketch

9.10 × 8.00 m between external wall centres. Width grid: 3.35 / 2.40 / 3.35 m. Rear rooms: 4.10 m centreline depth; bathroom: 2.60 m. Ground-floor doors and windows retain the sketch’s arrangement. The stair is revised in R02 as requested.

Your confirmed changes

External timber walls are 300 mm, replacing the sketch’s 240 mm. Outside dimensions become 9.40 × 8.30 m. The front covering projects 1.00 m from the outside front wall, with side-wall returns. Sage roof and warm spruce finish. R02 turns the sofa toward a wall-mounted TV beside the fireplace.

Raised roof & glazed loft

Ground-floor clear height 3.000 m; loft floor +3.235 m; eaves wall datum +5.235 m (2.000 m above the loft); roof pitch 30°. The calculated wall-centre ridge is +7.862 m. The upper suite has glazing and a small guarded landing overlooking the double-height living room.

To resolve before construction

The sketch’s 3.00 m roof-rise note / 8.235 m overall height does not agree with its 30° pitch and 9.10 m span. Roof build-up, structural members, fixings, glazing, stair/guard compliance, foundations and services require the appointed designers’ details. No additional 2 m is added on top of the sketch’s already raised roof.

R02 stair proposal: straight flight along the bedroom wall, 16 equal rises of 202.2 mm, 240 mm goings, approximately 40.1° pitch. Treads are 940 mm wide; clear width between handrails is approximately 846 mm. The 1.30 × 1.04 m landing and shifted loft doorway meet the new flight. Final stair, guard and stove installation details require the appointed designers’ confirmation.

Internal partitions: 165 mm. Timber courses: 310 mm. Furniture, fixtures, finishes and landscaping illustrate the brief. The small “balcony” is the internal loft landing, not an external cantilever. The original 2022 binder describes a smaller steel-frame house; this viewer uses the later timber sketch as you confirmed.
