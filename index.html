DESIGN BASIS · R04

What the builders are seeing.

This is a dimensional visual brief for discussion and estimating. It is not a structural, fabrication or construction drawing.

From the manufacturer’s sketch

9.10 × 8.00 m between external wall centres. Width grid: 3.35 / 2.40 / 3.35 m. Rear rooms: 4.10 m centreline depth; bathroom: 2.60 m. Ground-floor doors and windows retain the sketch’s arrangement. The original exterior walls are 240 mm. R04 preserves the sketch’s internal room sizes when increasing them to 300 mm.

Your confirmed changes

External timber walls are 300 mm, replacing the sketch’s 240 mm. All extra thickness grows outward, making the outside dimensions 9.46 × 8.36 m and revised wall-centre grid 9.16 × 8.06 m. The clear internal envelope remains 8.86 × 7.76 m, before internal partitions. The front covering projects 1.00 m from the outside front wall, with side-wall returns. Sage roof and warm spruce finish. R02 turns the sofa toward a wall-mounted TV beside the fireplace.

Raised roof & glazed loft

Ground-floor clear height 3.000 m; loft floor +3.235 m; eaves wall datum +5.235 m (2.000 m above the loft); roof pitch 30°. The calculated wall-centre ridge is +7.880 m. The upper suite has glazing and a small guarded landing overlooking the double-height living room.

To resolve before construction

The sketch’s 3.00 m roof-rise note / 8.235 m overall height does not agree with its 30° pitch and original 9.10 m span. The outward wall revision uses a 9.16 m span. Roof build-up, structural members, fixings, glazing, stair/guard compliance, foundations and services require the appointed designers’ details. No additional 2 m is added on top of the sketch’s already raised roof.

R04 stair proposal: two lower treads and a small quarter-turn landing lead to the upper flight along the bedroom wall. There are 17 equal rises of 190.3 mm; upper goings are 250 mm (approximately 37.3°), lower goings 260 mm. The 900 mm loft doorway moves 300 mm left. Treads remain 940 mm wide, approximately 846 mm clear between handrails. The upper landing remains 1.30 × 1.04 m. Final stair, guard and stove installation details require the appointed designers’ confirmation.

Internal partitions: 165 mm. Timber courses: 310 mm. Furniture, fixtures, finishes and landscaping illustrate the brief. The small “balcony” is the internal loft landing, not an external cantilever. The original 2022 binder describes a smaller steel-frame house; this viewer uses the later timber sketch as you confirmed.

 DRAWING REFERENCES
